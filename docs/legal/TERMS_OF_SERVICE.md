## 1. Acceptance
By creating a MeshPoke account you agree to these terms. You must be 18 or older and verify a Hong Kong (+852) mobile number.

## 2. What MeshPoke is (and is not)
MeshPoke is a social discovery app: a live map of spots (gatherings) hosted by users and venues.
- **We are not a booking agent, escrow, or payment processor for events.** Any arrangement about venue spend, splitting bills, or event tickets is solely between you, the host, and the venue.
- We never hold deposits for events. Subscription and pack purchases are the only payments we process.
- Venue listings and drop offers come from merchants; MeshPoke does not endorse venues or guarantee their conduct.

## 3. Your conduct
- One account per person, real identity. Phone verification is mandatory.
- No harassment, spam, scam solicitation, or moving users to external apps to defraud them. Links in notes never auto-render; suspicious patterns are flagged.
- Hosts are volunteer members, not our employees. Their decisions (who joins their spot) are theirs; abuse of host powers is reportable and moderated.
- Attend what you confirm. No-shows reduce your Show-up Score, which affects your ability to join spots that set a minimum.

## 4. Content & licence
You keep ownership of content you post (profile, photos, messages). You grant us a licence to host and display it for the service (e.g. showing your sketch in a room you join). Photos are never shown outside your connections.

## 5. Subscriptions, packs & passes

- Poke packs and passes are one-time, non-refundable digital consumables, except where Hong Kong law or the app stores require refunds.
- Free tier is permanent and usable. Paid tiers buy speed and convenience, never visibility: free users can always be seen, apply, and get accepted.

## 6. Merchants
Merchants claim venues with a valid Hong Kong Business Registration certificate, agree to accurate data (location, offers, prices), and to honour drops and ladder discounts they publish. Per-seat fees are billed monthly; disputes within 7 days via the merchant portal. Founding pricing terms apply year one.

Meeting new people carries risk. We provide verification tools (phone, venue QR check-ins, Show-up Score, ratings) but cannot guarantee anyone's conduct. Use the report/block tools; emergencies: call 999. You attend events at your own risk. To the maximum extent permitted by law, MeshPoke's liability is limited to the amount you paid us in the preceding 12 months.

## 8. Suspension & termination
We may warn, mute, suspend, or remove accounts for conduct violations, with audit records. You may delete your account anytime (Settings → Delete account).

## 9. Changes
We'll notify you in-app of material changes 14 days before they take effect.
