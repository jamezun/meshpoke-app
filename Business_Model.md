## 1. Pitch line and positioning

**Poke the mesh. Watch your network ripple.**
A live map for meeting people in real life.

**One-line business model:** a map-native social mesh where users pay for reach and comfort, merchants pay for verified foot traffic and retention tools, discovery runs on self-declared keywords — and the map's popularity signal stays activity-based, never pay-to-win.

### 1.1 Positioning: networking-first

MeshPoke is marketed as a **live networking map** — not a dating app, not a dinner-strangers app. Eating out is one venue type among many (drinks, hikes, board games, coffee chats, industry minglers); spots are **tools for meeting people**, not the product. Lower stakes than dating, more real than LinkedIn.

- **Primary (EN):** *Poke the mesh. Watch your network ripple.* — one touch spreads outward: poke → spot → crew → friends-of-friends.
- **Subline:** *A live map for meeting people in real life.*

**Alternates:**
- *Poke in. Mesh up. Network IRL.* (app-store subtitle)
- *Pull one thread. Poke the mesh. Find your network.* (brand storytelling)
- *Poke once, mesh forever.* (social bios)
- *The city is a mesh. Your network is one poke away.*
- *Poke the mesh. Meet the people.*
- *LinkedIn introduced you. We'll get you a table.* (professional jab)

**Visual system:** the ripple is the brand motif — logo mark, a pulse animation when a poke is sent, and spot pins that ripple as check-ins arrive.

**Elevator (app store / investors):** MeshPoke is a live map for meeting people in real life: join a spot by shared vibe — a dinner, a hike, a board-game night, an industry mingle — connect with the ones who click, and one-tap rebook the crew. Networking without the LinkedIn feed; nobody can ever pay to be more visible on the map.

**Pitch pillars:** networking without dating pressure · vibe-first, not looks · live map, not dead feed · any-activity spots · rebook-the-crew compounding · merchants earn prominence through check-ins, never payments · safety by design (group, public, verified).

| vs | They are | We are |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn | a feed of resumes and humblebrags | facetime — your network built in rooms, not comments |
| Timeleft | one curated stranger dinner on Wednesdays | any activity, any night, city-wide — and the crew rebooks |

**Practical consequences:** App Store category = **Social** (not Dating). Spot templates: Dinner · Drinks · Coffee · Hike · Run · Tennis · Pickleball · Badminton · Board games · Industry mingle · Open sport — no dinner default. Copy says **"connect"**, never "match". Users who find dates or friends are wins, not violations.

## 2. Revenue architecture

Five lanes; MeshPoke never holds or processes event money.

| Lane | Who pays | What they buy | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Consumer subscription** | Power users, socialites | Reach (pokes, applications), comfort (rooms, RSVPs), convenience (waitlist, drops, crews) | Growth + engagement |
| **B. Consumer passes and credits** | Casual and bursty users | 7-day pass, poke packs, filter pass | Low-friction upsell |
| **C. Merchant SaaS** | Restaurants, venues | Membership CRM, QR check-in, loyalty ladder, drops, coupons | Stable core revenue |
| **D. Per-seat drop fees** | Merchants | Verified guests who check in through a drop, priced by venue price band | Foot-traffic revenue; the stage-1 merchant value |
| **E. Sponsored placements** | Merchants, brands | Labelled drop amplification, branded pins | Secondary; always labelled |

**Rule:** logo size and map prominence come **only from verified member activity** — never from payment. Per-seat fees and sponsored placements buy labelled distribution, never marker size or ranking.

## 3. Discovery layer: keywords and vibe profiles

### 3.1 Profile (sketch — faceless until the photo ladder unlocks more)

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Industry | Dropdown: Finance / Tech / Design / Media / Medical / Legal / Education / Hospitality / Student / Other |
| Role one-liner | 40 chars, free text ("FX trader by day, board-game shark by night") |
| Keywords | One set of 10 per user, each with a self-declared level 1–10; max 3 at level 8+ |
| Vibe prompts | 3 short answers ("Perfect Friday night…", "Table topic I'll hijack…", "Ask me about…") |
| Verification badges | Liveness-verified selfie (required to apply or poke); optional work-email / LinkedIn industry badge |

- Brand line: **"poke by vibe, not looks."**
- Keywords may become **peer-verified** (2+ endorsements, weighted by the endorser's own level). Verified vs self-declared is shown to viewers.
- **The platform never gates anyone by keyword.** Keywords rank and filter; creators may require up to 3 keywords for their own spots.

### 3.2 Discovery (local-scoped, never global)

- Map and spot filters: "people here with **tennis ≥ 7**". Filters apply to map markers, room member lists and sketch cards.
- Vibe match % on spot cards and sketches ("87% — tennis, HKU").
- Hosts tag spots; joiners see relevance-ranked spots.
- **No global user search.** Spot search is scoped to the map view or district (HK's 18).
- Default map view = "this week", so the map never looks empty at off-peak times.

### 3.3 Trust layer

- "Friend of friend" badge (degree 2) on profiles and room members — a lightweight signal, not a browsable graph.
- No degree-based search, no paywall on degree depth at launch.

### 3.4 Why keywords, not a graph

- Every major dating app converged on interest filtering; graphs as *the product* are a graveyard (Shapr, Lunchclub consumer).
- Keywords give the map **relevance without breaking serendipity** — users still discover by proximity; keywords just rank.

- **We never meter browsing or joining as the path to connection.** Rooms are the primary social loop; free users get 5 rooms + 3 RSVPs + unlimited browsing. Pokes to strangers are metered because they are directed 1:1 outreach; pokes to people you have met are free for 48 hours.
- Free poke allowance (5/wk) ≈ Hinge-class signal allowance — playable weekly, scarce enough that Plus feels good.
- No suppression anywhere: free users appear in all inboxes, queues and rankings — **paid = faster, never hidden**.
- No personalised offers, ever. One published price per channel (§10).

## 5. Group formation, Show-up Score and crews

### 5.1 Spot states and creator-admin

Spot states: `forming → confirmed → live → done → crew`. Spots span dinners, drinks, coffees, hikes, runs, racket sports, board games and industry minglers — **the spot is a tool for meeting people; dinner is not the product**.

Creator-admin (democratised Timeleft ops):
- Sets name, required keywords (≤3), min Show-up Score, price range (**informational only — MeshPoke never handles event payments**), location (district anchor while forming; **venue pin required on confirm**), min/max headcount, date range, and optional women-only or gender-balanced setting.
- Reviews applications as **sketches only** (photo ladder intact).
- Removals are logged and reportable; creator abuse → hosting rights revoked (capacity is trust-based: 1 new → 3 proven → 5 top).

### 5.2 Date-on-fill (anti-shrinkage core)

- A spot forms without a date; **when min headcount is reached, the creator pins the date**; all members confirm within a **48h window** or the spot auto-dissolves.
- **Overbook buffer:** creator may accept above min to absorb flakes; bail → waitlist backfill.
- **24h bail grace** before event time; later bails = no-show record.

### 5.3 Show-up Score (money-free anti-flake teeth)

- Inputs: no-show / late-cancel rate, check-in streak, verification rate. Ground truth = **verified check-in (GPS + QR)**.
- New users show **"unproven"** — a state, not a penalty.
- Visible to creators on applications; creators may set minimum thresholds; **90-day decay**; enforcement ladder for repeat offenders.
- **Flake forgiveness:** member explains within 48h → creator accepts → record voided (kept in private log). No cap in v1.
- **Symmetry:** a creator's own last-minute cancel hits the creator's score + auto-apology to members.
- **No deposits.** Date-on-fill prevents shrinkage instead of compensating it.

### 5.4 Post-spot pokes (free for 48 hours)

No separate connect or follow feature — everything runs on pokes.
- When a spot is marked done, everyone who checked in (GPS + QR verified) sees a **"People you met"** list for 48 hours.
- Each poke button is labelled **"Free poke · met at [spot] · 31h left"** and does not use the weekly allowance.
- **Mutual pokes = connected**, as everywhere else. Either side can disconnect any time, silently.
- After 48 hours, poking someone from that spot costs a normal poke.
- Chatroom co-presence does **not** qualify — only verified check-in at the same spot.

### 5.5 Application queues and poke inbox (sorted, never hidden)

- Application = sketch + Show-up Score + shared keywords, sent to the creator.
- **Default sort = "Recommended"**: a weighted blend of tier boost (Pro > Plus > Free), vibe match and Show-up Score. The boost is weighted, never absolute — a strong free application can outrank a weak paid one.
- Creators (and inbox owners) can switch to **vibe match, Show-up Score, or newest**.
- Express badges and total counts always visible; a free application can be below the fold, never off it.
- Creators **accept/decline with reason** (one-tap presets + free text).
- Applications persist until the spot confirms or closes — no daily reapply.
- Monitored: free-tier acceptance rate must not decay (§11).

### 5.6 Crews and rebook

1. After a spot, every attendee sees **"Do it again?" → Keep crew / Leave**. Leaving is silent.
2. Those who keep become a **crew** with a small persistent chat.
3. Any crew member with hosting rights can tap **Rebook**: same venue and template, with a date poll for the crew.
4. Optionally open empty seats to the public.
5. A crew goes dormant after 60 days with no rebook.

Crews can link a WhatsApp group, but date polls, venue perks, check-in streaks, waitlist backfill and rebook stay in-app.

### 5.7 Host rewards

- **2 days of Pro per qualifying spot hosted.** Qualifying = reached min headcount, ≥80% of confirmed members checked in, average rating ≥4 stars.
- **Cap: 7 Pro days per month.** Earned days expire after 60 days.
- Optional merchant-funded host perks (e.g. a free drink for the host at the venue) — zero cost to MeshPoke.

## 6. Trust and safety (v1, all tiers, never paywalled)

Hong Kong loses tens of millions of HKD a month to romance and investment scams that start on dating apps, so safety ships in v1 rather than later.

| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Liveness-verified selfie badge | Required before applying to any spot or sending pokes; photos stay hidden per the photo ladder |
| Work-email / LinkedIn industry badge | Optional networking credibility without a résumé feed |
| Scam interstitials | Flag crypto, investment, "download this app" and move-to-Telegram patterns in DMs with a warning screen |
| Link lock | No off-platform links or contact handles in DMs before a first verified meet |
| Women-only / gender-balanced spots | Host settings for safer, balanced groups |
| Share-my-spot | Send venue, time and attendee count to a trusted friend |
| Private post-spot safety rating | Feeds trust signals and Show-up Score enforcement; never shown publicly |
| Report flow | One-tap report/block; links to HK Police Scameter for scam checks |
| Rate limits | Poke caps, 7-day expiry, trust-gated permissions for new accounts |

- Benchmark: HK loyalty/CRM systems typically cost ~HK$1,000–2,100/month plus setup fees, so Growth at HK$388 with no setup fee is a strong wedge.

### 7.2 Membership and discount mechanics

**Flywheel:** drops recruit strangers → membership keeps regulars → verified check-ins feed venue map size + CRM → merchant pays SaaS for retention tools → better perks keep members active.

- **Founding circle (social core):** merchant-assigned VIPs (min 2, max 25), never auto-expire.
- **Membership (loyalty layer):** cap per tier — Starter 25 · Growth 100 · Pro unlimited.
- **Marker size and ranking = verified check-in activity only** — never member count, never tier, never fees paid. Pins show activity ("42 check-ins this week"), never member totals.
- Regular seats kept by activity: no check-in for 60 days → auto-released to waitlist (Free: claim within 24h; Plus/Pro auto-join saved venues with 2×/3× weight).
- **Discount ladder:** merchant-configured steps (e.g. 3 visits → 10% · 5 → 15% · 10 → 20% or free item). Visit = QR check-in, GPS-verified, max 1/day. Cash ceiling ~15%; ladder decays one step after 60 days idle.
- **Discount drops:** count + discount + expiry ("25 spots, 15% off, ends Friday"); claim → QR redeem (proximity-gated); max ~3 active/week.
- **Member coupons:** unlock once a venue reaches its member threshold (default 50 members; merchant-configurable). Redeemed at the counter.

### 7.3 Per-seat drop fees (pay for verified guests)

Merchants pay a fixed fee per verified seat that checks in through a drop or sponsored placement. The fee scales with the venue's price band (~8% of typical per-person spend), so a café and a fine-dining room both pay a fair share without MeshPoke ever seeing a bill.

| Spend per person (HK$) | Fee per verified seat (HK$) | Example: table of 4 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–50 | 3 | HK$12 |
| 51–100 | 6 | HK$24 |
| 101–200 | 12 | HK$48 |
| 201–500 | 25 | HK$100 |
| 501–1,000 | 50 | HK$200 |
| 1,000+ | 80 | HK$320 |

Rules:
- **Only MeshPoke-driven seats are charged** — guests who claimed a drop or sponsored placement, then checked in. Organic regulars and members are covered by SaaS and never charged per seat.
- **Verified seat** = drop claimed first + GPS + rotating QR on the merchant's tablet + liveness-verified account older than 7 days. One charge per user per drop.
- **Band selection:** merchant picks a band at venue claim. MeshPoke cross-checks against OpenRice / Google Maps price ranges and an optional post-check-in question ("Roughly how much did you spend per person?"). If the median guest answer sits in another band for 30 days, the venue moves band with notice and a dispute window.
- **Monthly budget cap** set by the merchant; drops pause when the cap is reached.
- **Disputes:** merchant can dispute a seat within 7 days.

### 7.4 Billing and money flows

- Guests pay the merchant directly at the counter. **MeshPoke never touches guest money** — no ticketing, deposits, escrow or stored value.
- At month end MeshPoke charges the merchant's **card on file** for SaaS + verified seats from its own check-in logs (invoice detail per drop). No prepaid balances.
- Failed charge → drops pause until paid.
- Merchants or creators may paste their own booking or payment link on a spot; MeshPoke only displays it.
- All discounts and coupons are funded by merchant margin. MeshPoke never subsidises and takes no cut of the discount.

### 7.5 Founding merchant programme (cold start)

- First 10–15 venues per neighbourhood: **free Growth for 6 months** (QR adoption, feedback, case-study rights). Target ≥60% conversion at month 7.
- Venue claims verified with a Business Registration certificate.
- Founding consumer + merchant pricing follows the HK 開台價 convention — early supporters keep their rate while subscribed.

## 9. Unit economics (HKD, planning estimates)

At 20,000 MAU the model nets about HK$138,600/month at ~76% contribution margin, with merchants supplying roughly half of revenue; break-even on a HK$10k/month budget is ~118 consumer payers or ~27 Growth merchants.

Assumptions: 2.5% paid conversion (4% = upside case); 80/20 Plus/Pro; effective ARPU after founding and term discounts ≈ HK$105 Plus, HK$165 Pro; 50% of consumer revenue billed on web (~3% card fees) and 50% in-app (15% store commission) → ~9% blended; merchant revenue billed on web (~3%).

### 9.1 Steady state (20,000 MAU, one city)

| Line | Gross HK$/month | Net of fees HK$/month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consumer subscriptions: 400 Plus × 105 + 100 Pro × 165 | 58,500 | 53,200 |
| 7-day passes + packs + filter passes | 7,000 | 6,400 |
| Merchant SaaS: 150 merchants (60% Growth, 15% Pro) | 54,900 | 53,250 |
| Per-seat drop fees: 45 merchants × 40 seats × avg HK$12 | 21,600 | 20,950 |
| Sponsored amplification | 5,000 | 4,850 |
| **Total** | **≈147,000** | **≈138,650** |

Operating costs ≈ HK$27,000/month (infra 6k · trust & safety 12k · support 6k · liveness verification 3k; payment and store fees already deducted). **Contribution ≈ HK$111,600/month (~76% of gross).**

Upside case (4% conversion, 800 payers): consumer gross rises to ≈HK$104,800 and total gross to ≈HK$186,000.

### 9.2 Bootstrap floor (solo founder, HK$10k/month)

- **~118 consumer payers** at founding web prices (≈HK$85 net each), or
- **~27 Growth merchants** at list (≈HK$376 net) / ~36 at founding (≈HK$279 net), or
- a mix such as **60 payers + 18 founding Growth merchants ≈ HK$10,100/month**.

Merchant revenue is the floor: ~30 paying venues in one district covers the whole budget.

## 10. Pricing principles

1. **Flat, transparent, boring.** One published price per channel (web, in-app), no personalised checkout offers. Boring receipts, no surprise renewals, instant cancel.
2. **Paid = faster, never hidden.** Paid tiers get a weighted boost and badges in Recommended sorting; users can always re-sort, and free users are never removed from view.
3. **Creation by trust, joining by payment.** Hosting capacity is earned via Show-up Score, never sold; comfort caps are the paid tiers; good hosting earns capped Pro days.
4. **Never meter the path to connection.** Browsing, rooms and post-spot pokes stay free; only pokes to strangers are metered.
5. **Keywords rank; they don't gate.** The platform never gates by keyword; creators may for their own spots.
6. **Merchant revenue is the floor, consumer the ceiling.** SaaS and per-seat fees pay the bills; subscriptions fund growth.
7. **Start high, adjust with data.** List prices launch at HK$128 / 198; founding prices lock while continuously subscribed (開台價).
8. **Map prominence is earned only.** Check-in-verified activity, never member count, tier or fees paid.
9. **MeshPoke never touches guest money.** Merchants collect; MeshPoke bills merchants monthly from its own verified check-in logs.

**Key metrics**
