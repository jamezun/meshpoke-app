# Business Model & Pricing Model (v3 — HKD pricing · group formation · Show-up Score)

## 1. One-line business model

**A map-native social mesh where users pay for reach and comfort, merchants pay to retain, discovery runs on self-declared keywords — and the map's popularity signal stays activity-based, never pay-to-win.**

### 1.1 Positioning: **networking-first**

MeshPoke is marketed as a **live networking map** — not a dating app, not a dinner-strangers app. Eating out is one venue type among many (drinks, hikes, board games, coffee chats, industry minglers); spots are **tools for meeting people**, not the product. Lower stakes than dating, more real than LinkedIn.

**Primary (EN):** *"See who's out. Meet on purpose. Grow your circle."* — discover → mingle → network growth.

**Primary (HK launch, Canto):** *「成個城市，就係你嘅人脈。」* (The whole city is your network.)

**Alternates:**
- *LinkedIn introduced you. We'll get you a table.* (professional jab)
- *Feed less. Meet more.* (anti-app, ad-ready)
- *Dinner, drinks, or doubles — the crew forms on the map.* (any-activity/sports angle)
- *Your city is your network.* (ecosystem angle — shortest)
- *Networking that happens in real life — dinner's just the excuse.* (spot-as-tool angle)
- *人脈唔係connect，係見面。* (Canto LinkedIn jab)
- *人脈，原來就喺附近。* (Canto soft version — your network is closer than you think)

**Elevator (app store / investors):** MeshPoke is a live map for meeting people in real life: join a spot by shared vibe — a dinner, a hike, a board-game night, an industry mingle — connect with the ones who click, and one-tap rebook the crew. Networking without the LinkedIn feed; nobody can ever pay to be more visible on the map.

**Pitch pillars (what every pitch must hit):** networking without dating pressure · vibe-first, not looks · live map not dead feed · any-activity spots (dinner is a tool) · rebook-the-crew compounding · merchants earn prominence through check-ins, never payments.

**Positioning table:**

| vs | They are | We are |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | a feed of resumes and humblebrags | facetime — your network built in rooms, not comments |
| Timeleft | one curated stranger dinner on Wednesdays | any activity, any night, city-wide — and the crew rebooks |
| Dating apps | high-stakes looks market | zero-pressure mingling; romance isn't banned, it just isn't the frame |

**Practical consequences:** App Store category = **Social** (not Dating) — stigma-free, broader search reach. Spot-creation templates cover Dinner · Drinks · Coffee · Hike · Run · Tennis · Pickleball · Badminton · Board games · Industry mingle · Open sport — no dinner default. Copy says **"connect"** (mutual poke → connection), never "match" — mechanic unchanged, vocabulary de-dating-ified. Product stays a neutral mesh; users who find dates or friends on it are wins, not violations.

## 2. Revenue architecture (four lanes)

| Lane | Who pays | What they buy | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Consumer subscription** | Power users / socialites | Reach (pokes, applications), comfort (rooms, RSVPs), convenience (waitlist, drops) | Growth + engagement |
| **B. Consumer credits** | Casual users | Poke packs, filter passes | Low-friction upsell |
| **C. Merchant SaaS** | Restaurants / venues | Membership CRM, QR check-in, loyalty ladder, drops | **Stable core revenue** |
| **D. Sponsored placements** | Merchants / brands | Labeled drop amplification, branded pins | Secondary, must stay clearly labeled |

**Rule:** logo size and map prominence come **only from verified member activity** — never from payment. This protects trust in the map.

## 3. Discovery layer: keywords + vibe profiles

### 3.1 Profile (sketch — faceless until the photo ladder unlocks more)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Industry | dropdown: Finance / Tech / Design / Media / Medical / Legal / Education / Hospitality / Student / Other |
| Role one-liner | 40 chars, free text ("FX trader by day, board-game shark by night") |
| **Keywords** | **one set of 10 per user** (no per-mode sets), each with self-declared level 1–10; max 3 at level 8+ |
| Vibe prompts | 3 short answers ("Perfect Friday night…", "Table topic I'll hijack…", "Ask me about…") |

- Brand line: **"poke by vibe, not looks."**
- Keywords may become **peer-verified** (2+ endorsements, weighted by endorser's own level). Verified vs self-declared is shown to viewers.
- **Platform never gates anyone by keyword.** Keywords rank and filter; creators may *require up to 3 keywords* for their own spots.

### 3.2 Discovery (local-scoped, never global)

- Map & spot filters: "people here with **tennis ≥ 7**". Filters apply to map markers, room member lists, and sketch cards.
- Vibe match % on spot cards and sketches ("87% — tennis, HKU").
- Spot tags: hosts tag spots; joiners see relevance-ranked spots.
- **No global user search.** Spot search scoped to map view / district (HK's 18). Discovery stays location- and spot-scoped — that is the product.

### 3.3 Trust layer

- "Friend of friend" badge (degree 2) on profiles/room members — lightweight signal, not a browsable graph.
- No degree-based search, no paywall on degree depth at launch.

### 3.4 Why keywords, not a graph

- Every major dating app converged on interest filtering; graphs as *the product* are a graveyard (Shapr, Lunchclub consumer).
- Keywords give the map **relevance without breaking serendipity** — users still discover by proximity; keywords just rank.

Packs exist for **free users only in practice** — subscriber regen makes them redundant for Plus/Pro. Never double-charge.

### 4.3 Free-tier guardrails (the anti-Tinder/anti-CMB stance)

- **We never meter browsing or joining as the path to connection.** Rooms are the primary social loop; free users get 5 rooms + 3 RSVPs + unlimited browsing. Pokes are metered because they're *directed 1:1 outreach* (super-like class), not the swipe.
- Free poke allowance (5/wk) ≈ Hinge-class signal allowance — playable weekly, scarce enough that Plus feels good.
- No suppression anywhere: free users appear in all inboxes, all queues, all rankings — **paid = faster, never invisible-free**.
- No personalized offers, ever. One price for everyone (see §8).

### 4.4 Group formation & Show-up Score

**Spot states:** `forming → confirmed → live → done`. Spots span dinners, drinks, coffees, hikes, runs, tennis/pickleball/badminton games, board games, industry minglers — **the spot is a tool for meeting people; dinner is not the product**.

**Creator-admin (democratized Timeleft ops):**
- Sets: name, required keywords (≤3), min Show-up Score, price range (**informational only — MeshPoke never handles event payments**), location (district anchor while forming; **venue pin required on confirm** — QR check-in needs a real place), min/max headcount, date range.
- Reviews applications as **sketches only** (photo ladder intact); never browses full candidate profiles.
- Removals are logged and reportable; creator abuse → hosting rights revoked (capacity is trust-based: 1 new → 3 proven → 5 top).

**Date-on-fill (anti-shrinkage core):**
- Spot forms without a date; **when min headcount is reached, creator pins the date**; all members confirm within a **48h window** or the spot auto-dissolves (one re-verification of intent where it matters — no daily reapply chores).
- **Overbook buffer:** creator may accept above min to absorb flakes; bail → waitlist backfill.
- **24h bail grace** before event time; later bails = no-show record.

**Show-up Score (money-free anti-flake teeth):**
- Inputs: no-show/late-cancel rate, check-in streak, verification rate. Ground truth = **verified check-in (GPS + QR)**.
- New users show **"unproven"** — not a penalty, a state.
- Visible to creators on applications; creators may set minimum thresholds; **90-day decay** keeps it current; enforcement ladder for repeat offenders.

**Flake forgiveness + symmetry:**
- Member explains within 48h → creator accepts → record voided (stays in private log). No forgiveness cap in v1.
- **Creator's own last-minute cancel hits the creator's score** + auto-apology to members. Symmetry is the trust.

**No deposits at launch.** Date-on-fill *prevents* shrinkage instead of compensating it (Timeleft's no-show 6→2 worst complaint; billing rage eliminated). Deposits = Phase 3+ option only if confirmed-spot flake rate exceeds threshold.

### 4.5 Application queues (sorted, never hidden)

- Application = sketch + score + shared keywords, sent to creator.
- Creator queue ordering: **express (Pro) → Plus → Show-up Score → timestamp.** All applications always visible; we **reject absolute paid-first queues** (TennisGo's fatal flaw — free users invisible until all paid withdrawn).
- Creators **accept/decline with reason** (one-tap presets + free text) — copied from TennisGo, the pattern worth keeping.
- Applications persist until the spot confirms or closes — **no 24h reapply for free users** (freshness is handled by the 48h confirm window at date-pin; daily reapply is a chore mechanic that churns the users who create density).

| | **Starter** | **Growth** | **Pro** |

| **Annual** | — | HK$3,880/yr (2 months free) | HK$8,880/yr (2 months free) |
| Venue claim + profile + check-in stats | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Standing spots (own chatrooms)** | **3** | **10** | **Unlimited** |
| Event spots | ✓ unlimited | ✓ | ✓ |
| Founding circle (2–25, never expires) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Membership caps** | 25 | 100 | **Unlimited** |
| Discount ladder | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Discount drops | 1 active | ✓ | ✓ priority |
| Check-in CRM | aggregate only | full history | full + export |
| Sponsored drop amplification | — | per-drop | per-drop, priority placement |
| Map pin | standard (activity-ranked) | rich (menu/hours/photos) | rich + branded |

- **Standing spots = community surface (supply), so generous; the paywall is on tools: caps, CRM, drops, amplification.** Industry norm: Discord/Slack give rooms free and monetize data/people/tools; Circle meters spaces at 20 for $89/mo. A real venue has ~3 natural communities (run club / board night / happy hour) — Starter=3 lets them feel the membership caps before paying.
- **Merchants never join user chatrooms.** Merchant accounts operate their own spots + founding-circle lobby + 1 city room (business-labeled). They reach users via drops and amplification — never by roaming communities.
- **Why merchants pay:** identifiable repeat customers, retention data, member management — not logo size.

### 5.2 Membership & discount mechanics

**The flywheel:** drops recruit strangers → membership keeps regulars → verified check-ins feed venue map size + CRM data → merchant pays SaaS for retention tools → better perks keep members active.

**Member seats — two layers**
- **Founding circle (social core):** merchant-assigned VIPs (min 2, max 25), never auto-expire. The lobby room / "who's here" community — the social object that makes MeshPoke social, not a coupon app.
- **Membership (loyalty layer):** merchant's cap within tier headroom — Starter 25 · Growth 100 · Pro unlimited. Capacity is a classic SaaS lever; sell it freely.
- **The rule that never moves: marker size & ranking = verified check-in activity only — never member count, never tier.** Pins display activity ("42 check-ins this week"), never member totals.
- Regular seats kept by activity: no check-in for 60 days → auto-released to waitlist. Waitlist: seat opens → notified (Free: claim within 24h); Plus/Pro auto-join saved venues + weighted priority (2×/3×) — weighted, never absolute.

**Discount ladder (members-only loyalty)**
- Merchant-configured steps (e.g. `3 visits → 10% · 5 → 15% · 10 → 20% or free item`). Visit = QR check-in, GPS-verified, max 1/day. **Cash ceiling ~15%** — beyond that, perks protect merchant margin. Ladder decays one step after 60 days idle.

**Discount drops (acquisition campaigns)**
- Count + discount + expiry ("25 spots, 15% off, ends Friday"); claim → QR redeem (proximity-gated); max ~3 active/week.

**Money flows**
- All discounts funded by merchant margin. **MeshPoke never subsidizes, never takes a cut, never processes event payments** (no deposits, no escrow, no SVF). Booking/payment handled by creator/merchant off-platform.
- MeshPoke earns: SaaS tiers + labeled amplification. Marker size grows only from verified check-ins.

### 5.1 Founding merchant program (cold start)

- First 10–15 venues per neighborhood: **free Growth for 6 months** (QR adoption, feedback, case study rights). Target ≥60% conversion at month 7.
- Founding consumer + merchant pricing follows the HK 開台價 convention — early supporters keep their rate forever; it reads as generosity, not desperation.

## 7. Unit economics (HKD)

### Bootstrap floor (solo founder, HK$10k/mo budget)

- **~80 paying users ≈ HK$10,400/mo (avg ~HK$130)** → covers the *entire* monthly budget. That's 80 payers out of a few thousand MAU (2–4% conversion) — reachable within the first district.
- Merchant SaaS (even 10 Growth merchants = HK$3,880) turns the floor into runway.

### Steady-state illustration (20,000 MAU, one city)

- 800 paid (4%): ~640 Plus × 128 + ~160 Pro × 198 → **≈HK$113,600/mo**
- 150 merchants: 60% Growth + 15% Pro → **≈HK$54,900/mo**
- Packs + filter passes + amplification → **≈HK$15,000/mo**

**Total MRR ≈ HK$183,500 (~US$23.5k)** · costs at that scale ≈ HK$30k (infra 6k · payment fees ~3% 5.5k · trust & safety 12k · support 6k) → **~84% gross margin**.

**Break-even ≈ 80 payers.** Everything above is founder salary and growth.

## 8. Pricing principles (why this works)

1. **Flat, transparent, boring.** One price for everyone, published, no personalized checkout offers (CMB's dark pattern — rejected). Boring receipts, no surprise renewals, instant cancel.
2. **Paid = faster, never invisible-free.** Every paid advantage is speed/comfort/convenience; visibility itself is never for sale — on either side of the market.
3. **Creation by trust, joining by payment.** Hosting capacity is earned via Show-up Score (never sold — anti-Meetup); comfort caps (rooms/RSVPs/pokes) are the paid tiers.
4. **We never meter the path to connection.** Browsing and rooms stay free; only directed 1:1 outreach is metered (5/wk free). Tinder/CMB meter the wrong thing and churn users.
5. **Keywords rank; they don't gate.** Platform never gates by keyword; creators may for their own spots.
6. **Merchant revenue is the floor, consumer the ceiling.** SaaS pays the bills; subscriptions fund growth.
7. **Founding prices lock forever** (開台價) — early believers keep their rate; loyalty compounds.
8. **Map prominence is earned only.** Check-in-verified activity, never member count, never payment tier.

## 10. Key metrics to track

- **Consumer:** paid conversion %, poke acceptance rate, **rebook-the-crew rate (crews that meet 2+ times)**, spot completion rate (forming→done), 30-day retention.
- **Formation health:** confirmed-spot fill rate, show-up rate at confirmed spots, no-show rate by tier, flake-forgiveness usage, auto-dissolve rate.
- **Merchant:** founding→paid conversion, member repeat-visit rate, check-in volume, churn.
- **Trust:** report rate per 1k pokes, scam-pattern interstitial triggers, queue-position vs acceptance fairness (free-tier acceptance rate must not decay).

## 11. Risks & pricing fixes

| Risk | Fix |
|---|---|
| Stingy free tier churns users (Tinder/CMB trap) | Browsing + rooms free; 5 pokes/wk; no suppression anywhere (§4.3) |
| Billing rage (Timeleft/CMB pattern) | Flat transparent pricing, boring receipts, instant cancel, no personalized offers |
| No-shows shrink events (Timeleft 6→2) | Date-on-fill + 48h confirm + overbook buffer + Show-up Score — prevention, not compensation |
| Paid queue dominance alienates free users | Sorted-never-hidden queues with visible counts; weighted, never absolute priority |
| Poke spam / harassment | Rate limits, trust-gated permissions, 7-day expiry, scam interstitials, one-tap report/block |
| Merchants game check-ins | GPS proximity + 1/day + anomaly flags; decay punishes idleness |
| Marker size seen as pay-to-win | Never sold; check-in-verified only; member counts never displayed |
| Keyword inflation / spam | Max 10, max 3 at level 8+, peer-verification weighting, verified-vs-declared shown |
| Scope creep (global search, degree graphs) | Discovery stays district/spot-scoped; explicitly out of scope until multi-city density |
| Stale competitor benchmarks | User-verified store checkouts only (§4.6 warning) |

---

**Bottom line:** Charge users for **reach and comfort**, never for visibility. Charge merchants for **retention tools**, never prominence. Keep the map's signal **earned**. Flat prices, founding loyalty, and a free tier that never becomes a trap.
